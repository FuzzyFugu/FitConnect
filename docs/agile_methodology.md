# FitConnect — Agile Methodology Selection

## Choice: Scrum (with a Kanban-style board)

We selected **Scrum** as the primary framework, visualised on a Kanban-style
board. The reasoning:

- **Fixed deadline, evolving requirements.** An 8-week window with a startup
  client whose priorities shift favours Scrum's timeboxed sprints and regular
  re-planning over a purely continuous flow.
- **Defined roles suit a 4-person team.** Scrum's Product Owner and Scrum Master
  roles map cleanly onto our team, giving clear accountability for *what* is built
  (PO) and *how* the team works (SM).
- **Built-in stakeholder feedback.** Sprint reviews give FitConnect a working
  increment every two weeks — essential for attracting early adopters and sponsors.
- **Kanban board for flow.** We kept a visible `To Do → In Progress → In Review →
  Done` board (Trello) to limit work-in-progress and surface blockers daily.

We considered **pure Kanban** (rejected: no cadence to force stakeholder demos)
and a **Hybrid/Scrumban** (partially adopted: we use Scrum ceremonies but a
continuous board). This pragmatic blend is common in small teams (Kniberg, 2015).

## Ceremonies used
- **Sprint planning** (start of each weekly sprint) — pull from prioritised backlog.
- **Daily stand-up** (15 min, async on busy days) — yesterday / today / blockers.
- **Sprint review** — demo the increment to the PO / stakeholder.
- **Retrospective** — inspect and adapt the process (see `retrospectives.md`).
